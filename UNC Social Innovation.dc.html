COMMUNITY · UAE / NEPAL · 2016–2018

UNC Social Innovation

A 9-day Social Innovation Bootcamp, June 2–10, 2017, ICA Training Centre, Lalitpur, Nepal.

[ PHOTO — community-unc: Nepal bootcamp participants ]

"ME=WE, Cultivating Change" — two Dubai-based art and design educators used creative, experiential learning to connect and empower youth in Nepal after the 2015 earthquake, with an international team of volunteers over three years. Youth from both countries built relationships that outlasted the program — several became entrepreneurs.
