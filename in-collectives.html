How the network grows

IN grows through shared practice. Those who continue the journey may evolve into IN-Collectives — opening the studios their own communities need, and reshaping ME=WE to fit their place.

01 · Equal partners

Not branches or franchises. Each collective is an equal partner that reshares its experience and learning, keeping the connected flow of practice alive.

02 · Locally rooted

Open the studios your place needs and run them in your own language and context. Every local insight feeds the network's shared evolution.

03 · Self-sustaining

Collectives build their own revenue through workshops and collaboration — autonomy and shared growth, held together with care.
