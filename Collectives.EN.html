IN-Collectives

IN grows with independent partners and collaborators, called IN-Collectives. We are the team behind the workshops — a close-knit group of like-minded people who want to bring out the best in themselves and society. Working in their own communities across diverse fields — teaching, hosting workshops, developing local initiatives — each brings ME=WE into their own context. Each IN-Collective contributes learning back into the wider studio network, allowing the work to grow through shared experience. We are all about spreading joy, sparking creativity, and helping one another make the world a better place.

"So, let's roll up our sleeves and co-design our future together, with the wisdom of experience and the curiosity of youth guiding our way. Welcome to INNOCO, where our collective story is just beginning."— Yunsun Chung
