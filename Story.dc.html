STORY

Stories told by everyone. Owned by no one.

Everyone knows the power of a story. It's the oldest gift within our DNA we share — one person's experience, offered so others can see further.

We gather them here because none of us sees the whole picture alone. Every perspective is partial by nature, so your story — exactly as you can tell it today — holds something the rest of us can't see without you. There are no spectators here to assess, only to appreciate.

I NOTICED IT

ME=WE I saw in the world: a performance, nature, a proverb, a film, a street. My noticing is the story.

The Four Beats — Every Story
1One moment, one place — set the scene.
2The dare, or the thing you noticed — what happened.
3What moved — in you, or between people.
4The name — why this is ME=WE to you.
