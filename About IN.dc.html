ABOUT IN

A small studio with a long question.

IN (INNOCO) is a small studio built on one idea: personal growth and the wellbeing of the people around us aren't separate things. We call this ME=WE.

Everything we do — workshops, projects with our neighbors, the way we teach — is a way of practicing that idea, not just talking about it.

How We Practice It

We run workshops built around the ME=WE Arc — head, heart, and hand working together, through nature, science, art, and social action. Möbius Making is where most people begin. We also take on real projects with our neighbors, schools, and communities. Both are how we learn — not from theory, but from doing something together and paying attention to what actually shifts.

We don't come with fixed answers. We come with a posture of learning, across generations and fields, looking to nature, science, art, and social action for a common language. The forces that divide us — competition, materialism, isolation — don't have to be the only way. We think we can find another way, by looking, listening, and making, side by side.

The IN Network

IN isn't built to be one centralized organization. It grows through shared practice. Those who continue the journey may become IN-Collectives — independent partners who carry this work forward and host their own studios, in their own local contexts, in their own way.

The Question We Carry

What kind of ancestor do I want to be?

This isn't a question for elders alone — it's for everyone, at any age. We're all bridges between who came before us and who comes next. IN exists to strengthen those connections, so no one carries this work alone.
