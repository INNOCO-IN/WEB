ABOUT IN

A practice that kept traveling.

Before IN had a name, it had a question — and that question kept finding new rooms. It began in Dubai, moved through Nepal, crossed to Canada, and now continues in Seoul. Each place added something. None of it was planned.

The Thread, In Order

Taiwan — Shadow Shifter. Now — Seoul.

Research & Recognition

The ME=WE framework has been shared through peer-reviewed work and international forums: a Springer handbook chapter, "Self, Society, Service in ME=WE" (2024); "Co-Designing a Pathway Through Food Revolution for Social Change" (2021); "Co-designing a social innovation model for changemakers" (2018); and a youth-empowerment case study (Banff / RSD, 2015). Presented at Dubai EXPO 2020 and the Parliament of World Religions 2022.
