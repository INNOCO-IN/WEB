Fifteen minutes. One honest moment. A story is a free gift — your experience, offered so others can see further. It doesn't need to be polished; it needs to be honest. Only steps 1 and 5 are required.

Step 1 — Your story · required

Two doors, not two rigid boxes. Pick whichever pulls you; if a story sits on the line, it belongs to whichever the teller feels.

I lived it

A moment I was inside. I acted, I risked, I was changed. I'm the one who dared.

I noticed it

ME=WE I saw in the world: a performance, nature, a proverb, a film, a street. My noticing is the story.

Stuck?

Think of one moment, one place. What was dared, or what you noticed. What moved — in you, or between people. Then: why this is ME=WE to you. Fifteen minutes is enough.
