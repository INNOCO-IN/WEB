Story Submission

It can be a few honest sentences, or a drawing, a photo, a film, a piece of music. Start with a short description of the moment — then bring whatever form carries it best. Only steps 1 and 5 are required. Thank you for adding your light.

Step 1 — Your story · required

Two doors — “I lived it,” or “I noticed it.”

Two doors, not two rigid boxes. Pick whichever pulls you; if a story sits on the line, it belongs to whichever the teller feels. 

I lived it

A moment I was inside. I acted, I risked, I was changed. I'm the one who dared.

I noticed it

ME=WE I saw in the world: a performance, nature, a proverb, a film, a street. My noticing is the story.

If you're not sure where to start — four gentle beats
1The moment — where were you, and when?
2What happened — what you did, or what you noticed?
3What moved — in you, or between people?
4Why — why is this ME=WE to you?

Fifteen minutes is enough. Skip any beat that doesn't fit.
