UAE Youth Camp

UAE Youth Cycle — a Social Innovation Bootcamp, in partnership with Sheraa.

[ PHOTO — community-uae: the Sheraa group photo ]

Over six months, young people in the UAE each set out to turn something they cared about into a real social enterprise — learning by doing, not by lecture. They found their projects through conversations in their own communities, shaped them into ideas, and tested them again and again with the people they hoped to serve. What came out of it wasn't a set of assignments — it was real ventures, real pitches, real people discovering they could build something.
