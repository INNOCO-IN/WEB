METANOIA

Change from within.

A four-day intensive for people whose inner condition shapes a community — educators, directors, anyone who holds space for others.

[ PHOTO — workshop-metanoia: a quiet, inward moment ]

METANOIA takes its name from the Greek word for change from within. Over four days, you step out of routine and look again — at yourself, the people around you, and the organization you serve. METANOIA draws on Theory U, alongside IN's own practice.

The Journey — Four Movements
SELF
Look inward, honestly.
RELATIONSHIP
See the people around you anew.
ORGANIZATION
Bring the shift into your work.
INTEGRATION
Carry it home as a way of living.

Co-created with Conrad Saldanha, and shaped by every group that has practiced it.

Bring METANOIA to your organization. Every program begins with a consultation.
