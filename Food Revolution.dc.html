Food Revolution

KULNA / Food Revolution for Social Change — a 4-year federally-funded transdisciplinary action research project.

[ PHOTO — community-kulna: the KULNA garden, students working ]

"Co-designing Food Revolution for Social Change" explored how to shift individual and collective wellbeing within a food system. KULNA Community Garden ("All of Us" in Arabic) became a physical sanctuary to grow food and bring students and faculty together — a social lab of hands-on workshops, lectures, and community activities. A classroom experiment became a living ecosystem, and produced multiple academic papers.
