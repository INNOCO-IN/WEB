The rules on this page
  • Tag rail — rotates 90° clockwise (reads downward), dot at the top. Dot color = tag text color (one color, never contrasting).
  • Type locked — tag Archivo 700/12px · eyebrow Archivo 700/13px (same color as tag) · title Newsreader 700/30px, line-height 1.0 · body Newsreader 400/17px, line-height 1.3.
  • Image — 4:3 frame, any photo auto cover-crops to fill. Identical shape on every card.
  • Spacing — fixed eyebrow → title → body gaps, same on every card. Equal card height; body clamped to 2 lines for a clean bottom edge.
  • Arrow — filled ink #2E3B40 circle, paper glyph, no border, bottom-right. Link sits on the arrow only so all text stays editable.
  • Corners square. Background = warm supporting tint of the page color; no two adjacent cards share a color.
  • Two types — (1) Content card: one Topic tag. (2) Story card: Topic on the rail + Format pictogram top-right of the image (Writing · Drawing · Photo · Video · Music · Dance).
  • States — Active: full color, arrow enabled. Inactive/Archived: desaturated + muted, no arrow.
